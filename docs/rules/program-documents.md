# Program document rules

These are the exact rules, bounds and formats behind PROGRAM-003, PROGRAM-005 and PROGRAM-006 in [CONTRACT.md](../../CONTRACT.md). The contract holds the requirement and its acceptance; this page holds the mechanism they rely on, as recorded when the contract was rewritten to the RER contract standard on 28 October 2026.

**Current semantics (22 October 2026):** Program → Documents lists the documents of the program's framework (SOC 2 system description; ISO Statement of Applicability, clause 6.1 risk assessment and treatment plan, clause 9.2 internal-audit programme and clause 9.3 management review; HIPAA risk analysis, policy set and business associate agreement inventory; PCI cardholder-data-environment scope, SAQ selection and worksheet, and Attestation of Compliance handoff). The owner generates each once; every generation, owner edit, input change and reconciliation writes a new immutable revision from live program state, and an approval names an approver from People, an effective date and a review deadline (the accountable owner's cycle: 12 months by default, 1 to 12 months by owner choice), kept beside the revision it approved. Pre-filled statements cite their source record and exact revision (program scope revision, inventory resource counts with the latest update, published policy version, vendor and vendor-document digest, risk and treatment records); a section that records cannot support stays marked for the owner and blocks approval until the owner writes it. Every document exports as PDF, DOCX and XLSX (a real WordprocessingML, SpreadsheetML or PDF file) with a document-control table and the statement that Evidence does not sign, attest or certify; each export is audited with its revision. A document's facts are compared with live state: a difference flags the approved document as needing review, naming each item (an SoA exclusion that no longer matches the applicability decision, a control marked implemented that no longer has current proof and passing tests, a changed inventory count or policy version, a BAA that changed state), and a reconciliation writes a new revision; the approved revision stays retrievable and exportable. Implementation status in the SoA is derived, never typed: implemented needs every mapped control's proof current and any mapped test passing now. The HIPAA risk analysis adds the shipped ePHI scenarios from inside the document as RISK-001 risks the owner can change; its BAA inventory and ePHI flows come from VENDOR-001. The SAQ type is selected from recorded answers (the most demanding channel decides); Each SAQ worksheet lists, requirement by requirement, the PCI DSS v4.0.1 requirement ids that SAQ's current revision assesses (Evidence's mapping, named with the SAQ revision it was read from), in Evidence's wording with that revision's response choices and the explanation each choice needs; other requirements are not shown. The PCI SSC text is licensed and not shipped: the merchant or QSA confirms the worksheet against the licensed SAQ, and the worksheet and AOC handoff say so. A new SAQ revision arrives as a reviewable library revision that flags approved worksheets for review. The January 2025 SAQ A revision is library revision 3; the installation keeps its earlier accepted SAQ revision until its owner reviews and accepts that change. Response choices follow the selected revision, with explanations required for not applicable, CCW, not tested (SAQ D only) and not in place; Evidence checks presence and the merchant or QSA judges adequacy. Diagram exhibits are retained originals with their digest. ISO audits and management reviews are recorded with their independence statement, nonconformities and corrective actions, and required clause 9.3 inputs. Document tables and exhibits join the custody archive. PROGRAM-004 inherits the document model, drift review and no-attestation obligations; AUDIT-001 and TRUST-001 inherit exports as the assessor's source.
