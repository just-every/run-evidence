# Failure-notice rules

These are the exact rules, bounds and formats behind FOLLOWUP-002 in [CONTRACT.md](../../CONTRACT.md). The contract holds the requirement and its acceptance; this page holds the mechanism they rely on, as recorded when the contract was rewritten to the RER contract standard on 28 October 2026.

**Current partial slice (21 October 2026):** A failing observation that opens or reopens a REMEDY-001 obligation notifies its accountable owner on the next five-minute scheduler run, on the channel that person chose under FOLLOWUP-001, without waiting for the SLA to approach. The notice names the test, failing item, control, mapped framework requirements, owner, severity, REMEDY-002 due date (UTC calendar date), the first how-to-remediate step (the shipped catalogue's first provider console step, or the owner-written step on a custom test), a link to the obligation and, where a WORK-001 ticket exists, its key and link. An item with no owner (or whose owner left) goes to the control owner, then the escalation contact, then the installation owner, and the notice says which. A test that fails on several items sends one notice per recipient listing each item with its own due date. Each delivery is recorded on the obligation per failure cycle and recipient with its outcome and reason (accepted, failed or uncertain) and is never resent; business-calendar quiet hours defer a notice until the window opens and a paused obligation stays silent. Reassigning or assigning an item notifies the new owner on the next run, and a regression after a verified closure opens a new cycle and a new notice. A verified closure is announced to whoever heard of the failure. The private obligation page (owner sign-in required) offers Acknowledge, which records who and when and never closes the obligation, and Reassign, which needs a reason and records it. Until delegated roles exist (AUTH-001) the signed-in owner session acts for the assigned person, and the history says so; only a verified re-test closes an obligation. Per ct (21 October 2026) the in-channel step is a private link, not a Slack interactive message or Teams bot. Gaps keeping this partial: Acknowledge and Reassign from inside the Slack or Teams message (needs a Slack app interactivity endpoint or a Teams bot); in-place update of an earlier message on reassignment, re-test or verification (a new message is posted instead, and a Teams workflow URL cannot edit); live Slack and Teams sandbox acceptance. No provider verification is claimed.
