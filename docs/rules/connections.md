# Connection custody and the private collector

These are the exact rules, bounds and formats behind CONNECT-001 in [CONTRACT.md](../../CONTRACT.md). The contract holds the requirement and its acceptance; this page holds the mechanism they rely on, as recorded when the contract was rewritten to the RER contract standard on 28 October 2026.

The installable private-network door is the fixed read-only Git collector (`docs/PRIVATE-COLLECTOR.md`): an owner reviews and registers its exact profile, it reports only repository label, branch/head counts, commit ID/time and bare state, and its receiving credential is retained as SHA-256 only, displayed once, connection-bound, rotatable and revocable. Only successful reads become source-observed; unsupported hosted policies stay unknown with manual evidence required, unreadable sources retain their denominator as unknown, and restored collectors remain inactive until fresh owner authorization. Source read timestamps must be within one hour, with at most five minutes of clock skew; repeated/older reads are refused. Existing observation cadence makes reads older than 1.5 times the test cadence plus one hour unknown; failures do not refresh a pass.
