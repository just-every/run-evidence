# Policy-tailoring rules

These are the exact rules, bounds and formats behind AGENT-006 in [CONTRACT.md](../../CONTRACT.md). The contract holds the requirement and its acceptance; this page holds the mechanism they rely on, as recorded when the contract was rewritten to the RER contract standard on 28 October 2026.

Current AGENT-006 semantics: local deterministic preparation uses the exact owner-selected customer requirement, imported framework/regulatory requirement, approved policy revision, recorded inventory rows and current risk method. When no control hint is supplied, it recommends an existing control by shared terms across the customer passage, selected framework material and actual inventory; this is a proposal of applicability, never proof of equivalence. It retains passages and source doors, assumptions, ambiguity, an ISO deadline only after an explicit by/due/no-later-than phrase in the passage, and the exact proposed control mapping, policy diff and scored risk assumptions. It distinguishes a contract promise from a framework requirement and asserts no legal interpretation is authoritative. Each decision names a person from People as reviewer and the exact proposal hash; that person must be the installation owner or hold administrator or compliance manager for the whole business (AUTH-001), their name is attribution and never a new authentication grant, and a delegated session cannot decide a proposal it prepared. Approval applies the mapping, an unassigned risk assessment, structured obligation and a new policy draft atomically; creator/assessment attribution names the signed-in actor, with the named reviewer retained separately in the decision; publication remains the existing separate POLICY-001 approval/publication door.

Source or inventory drift requires fresh preparation. Rejection creates none of those records. No source passage authorizes outbound action or work assignment.
