# Vendor diligence rules

These are the exact rules, bounds and formats behind VENDOR-001 in [CONTRACT.md](../../CONTRACT.md). The contract holds the requirement and its acceptance; this page holds the mechanism they rely on, as recorded when the contract was rewritten to the RER contract standard on 28 October 2026.

**Current semantics (22 October 2026):** A supplier has a profile (service, website, contact, data it can access from PHI, cardholder data, personal data, credentials and confidential business data, hosting region, annual cost in USD, renewal date, risk tier and the basis for it, and its subprocessors); every profile change is a numbered revision with the owner's reason. The tier is suggested from criticality and data and the owner may set another. Diligence the profile makes necessary (SOC 2 or ISO 27001 assurance for medium and high tiers, a BAA when PHI is recorded, a DPA when personal data is, a questionnaire for medium and high tiers) is listed as missing, requested, overdue, received, expired or verified. Prerequisites such as the BAA and regional controls for a non-US region are shown as "not verified by Evidence"; Evidence never signs or certifies for either party. Requests go out on one expiring supplier link per issue (at most 90 days, shown once, only its SHA-256 digest kept, emailed only when the owner ticks the explicit email choice). The token is the credential: supplier pages check no Origin (they send no referrer, so posts carry Origin: null) but refuse a cross-site Sec-Fetch-Site request, are served no-store with no referrer, never write the token to the audit, link events, logs or archive, and the owner closes a link at any time. The link shows only the requests it names; any unknown, closed, expired or other-vendor request answers the same plain 404 and is recorded against the link. Supplier documents (10 MiB each) are stored as immutable originals with their digest and read back before they are recorded; a newer document of the same kind supersedes the earlier one beside it, with a comparison of size, dates and bytes, and a changed or missing original is refused on download. Supplier answers are kept per question (yes, no or not applicable, with a comment) and a revision is appended beside the earlier answer; a final submission needs every question answered and the respondent's unverified name and email. Documents, answers and marketing claims are supplier-provided and unverified until the owner reviews a document as verified; a claim is verified only by a verified document of that vendor. Findings cite a document or an answer; accepting one, and approving past an open finding or missing diligence, is an exception that needs a named approver from People. An approval is append-only: approved, conditional (the condition and a future expiry; the owner records when it was met) or declined, with a reassessment date (default six months for the high tier and twelve otherwise, capped at the contract renewal). Reminders go to the vendor's business owner (or the installation owner) once per cycle: reassessment 14 days ahead and when overdue, a lapsed condition and an overdue request. Licensed questionnaires (ct 22 Oct 2026). Evidence ships the SIG Lite, SIG Core and CSA CAIQ v4 template types: question ids, domains, scoping and answer forms (SIG yes/no/not applicable with comment; CAIQ yes/no/not applicable, SSRM control ownership and implementation description). Their licensed text loads only by importing the owner's licensed workbook (SIG of a named year; CAIQ v4.0.x), which keeps every id, domain and scoping rule, records edition, licensee and confirming person, and refuses any SIG or CAIQ workbook without that confirmation whatever its name. A supplier may answer by uploading its completed workbook of the same edition; answers are kept per question id and stay unverified. The import maps the question worksheet's columns, preserves the original row values and explicit scoping rule/decision, and records its fingerprint. A scoped workbook comes from its licensed authoring tool; Evidence does not execute Excel macros. Up to 10,000 questions and 10 MiB are admitted. Out-of-scope ids remain in the request without requiring an answer. Until CSA or Shared Assessments licenses Evidence to ship their text, Evidence's own questionnaire is 'CCM v4 control questionnaire (Evidence wording)', never 'CAIQ'. Supplier-link digests never leave an installation: a restored link is closed and cannot authenticate. VENDOR-002 and VENDOR-003 inherit the document store, the unverified/verified distinction and the named-approver rule; ERASE-001 owns erasure of supplier contact data and answers.

Status note: None for current doors. Licensed text is import-only; workbook limits and scoped-authoring semantics are documented in `docs/QUESTIONNAIRE-WORKBOOKS.md`. VENDOR-002 and VENDOR-003 inherit the document store and verified/unverified distinction.
