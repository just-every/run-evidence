The projections contain names, receipt fingerprints, availability, periods and gaps. They exclude exhibit bodies, provider credentials, storage keys and bearer values. Imported labels and source text remain untrusted. Preparing a proposal exposes nothing to its reviewer.

Business credentials bind their signed subject through `bs_settings.owner_id` or `bs_team.business_id` to a native operator ID. The bootstrap owner is the installation owner. Email claims never select an account. Current native status and dedicated-auditor admission are re-read. A local promotion cannot become a Business editor.

Folder preparation additionally requires the existing possessed, unexpired, unrevoked prepare-only native credential. Its MCP agent ID and client ID are the original native grant ID; its sponsor is the grant's native creator. A Business credential alone returns `native_credential_required` for preparation; it cannot mint or select that grant. Business credentials can inspect the owner's metadata under policy. No native approval is impersonated.
