# Operating Evidence

OPS-001 covers every operation shipped today. Use the documented `rer setup`, `rer dev`, `rer build` and deployment commands in README.md and SEED-COMMAND-CONTRACT.md. Future operations in COLLECTION-001, CLOUD-002, CODE-001, APPS-001, MONITOR-001, HRIS-001, DEVICE-001, VULN-001, ISSUES-001, LIFECYCLE-001, AUTH-001 and AGENT-002..007 inherit these requirements before shipping.

Before private HTTP doors or Cron work touch data, Evidence checks DB, EXHIBITS, AUTH_ORIGIN, OWNER_EMAIL and SESSION_SECRET. An optional SETUP_TOKEN is checked when supplied. Configured mail checks MAIL_PROVIDER, MAIL_FROM, MAIL_API_KEY and the Cloudflare provider's MAIL_ACCOUNT_ID. Invalid settings produce HTTP 503 with only their names; Cron refuses them before dispatch. No raw provider/storage exception is published as an operation outcome.

Open Operations in the owner navigation (`/app/operations`, JSON `/api/operations`). It shows connector state and last successful read, pending/uncertain delivery and running/attention attempt counts, recorded exhibit bytes, a bounded R2 listing and whether that listing is truncated. Storage failures refuse the health read with 503. These measurements do not certify every object or provider. Backup status shows the last completed restore and explicitly says an external backup copy is not verified. Follow Yours to export and retain a complete archive outside this installation, and periodically restore it into an empty installation. Provider configuration pages itemize optional costs in USD; Evidence itself costs USD 0.

A Cron invocation has one 60-second elapsed wall-clock budget across its steps. Every step claims in D1 before work. One running claim per step prevents overlap. Scheduled and finish timestamps use the dispatch clock; the execution budget uses elapsed wall time. Successful or failed results are permanent; counts measure processed work, never successful controls or delivered messages. The owner may cancel a running step. Cancellation, budget expiry and lost claims stop further effects and leave Unknown, including when a network answer arrives late. D1 product statements execute atomically with a running-claim fence. Provider requests carry cancellation signals; R2 calls check the claim before and after dispatch. An already-dispatched external request or R2 write may have completed before cancellation; it cannot authorize subsequent product writes. Inspect underlying records before retrying. The scheduler returns without starting further steps once its budget expires; their durable product obligations remain due for the next tick.

An abruptly killed process leaves a running claim. At the inclusive 15-minute lease boundary, the next Cron tick records Unknown. An older worker cannot overwrite the result or write through its fenced product database. Recovery never fabricates source observations. Pending deliveries at least 15 minutes old become uncertain, at most 100 per tick, with an audit record and no automatic resend. The existing follow-up resolution door records later confirmation.

The newest 100 attempts are shown with a `more` indicator. Every retained operation attempt is included in the complete streaming custody archive. Terminal attempts restore unchanged; a running attempt restores as Unknown with an explicit interrupted-at-backup explanation, never as executable work. Earlier v10 archives without operation history remain supported. Original receipts, observations, immutable history and retained migrations continue to survive upgrades and restore. Source, mail, ticket and schedule grants remain inactive until explicitly reauthorized after restore.

Public doors refuse exhibits over 10 MiB, import ZIPs over 20 MiB, population CSVs over 5 MiB and archive parts over 1 MiB. Streaming archives have no total size ceiling. Neither refusal nor a paginated display truncates stored evidence. Storage/hosting plan allowances are the owner's provider limits; review USD estimates and current provider pricing before enabling them.
